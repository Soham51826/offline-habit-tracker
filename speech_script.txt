"Thank you, Soham. Respected faculty, I will now present our technical architecture and technology stack.

To achieve strict separation of concerns, we implemented a clean, layered Model-View-ViewModel (MVVM) architecture using Flutter and the Provider state management pattern. Our codebase in the `lib/` directory is divided into four distinct layers:

1. The Presentation Layer houses our views and ViewModels—specifically `AuthViewModel` for persistent local authentication and onboarding, and `HabitViewModel`, `InsightsViewModel`, and `StatsViewModel`. ViewModels extend `ChangeNotifier` and expose immutable state streams to reactive widgets.
2. The Domain Layer contains our pure business logic and behavioral heuristics, keeping calculation algorithms strictly decoupled from Flutter UI widgets.
3. The Data Layer implements the Repository Pattern on top of SQLite and persistent local preferences, isolating raw SQL queries and session storage behind clean domain interfaces.
4. The Background Services Layer bridges native Android APIs.

We chose Flutter 3.44 and Dart because they compile directly to native ARM machine code on Android, delivering smooth 60 and 120 FPS rendering. For visualization, we integrated `fl_chart`, for local session storage we used `shared_preferences`, and for background scheduling, we utilized the Android WorkManager API. Let us look closer at how our data layer guarantees relational integrity."


[SLIDE 5: SQLITE DATABASE SCHEMA & DATA INTEGRITY]
Target Duration: ~1 minute 10 seconds

Key Emphasis Points:
• Walk through the 3 primary tables: `habits`, `habit_logs`, and `behavioral_insights`.
• Highlight the composite index and `UNIQUE(habit_id, date)` constraint.

Verbatim Spoken Script:
"Because our application operates completely offline, the database schema is the single source of truth. We designed three primary relational tables in SQLite:

First, the `habits` table stores habit definitions—including title, category, target count, unit, scheduled frequency, reminder time, and an `anchor_habit_id` foreign key that enables procedural habit stacking.

Second, the `habit_logs` table records daily execution data—including completion status, numeric counts, mood scores from 1 to 5, and reflection notes. Notice the critical database constraint: `UNIQUE(habit_id, date)`. This constraint guarantees idempotency: even if a user rapidly toggles check-ins, duplicate historical logs can never corrupt the metrics.

Third, the `behavioral_insights` table caches generated behavioral nudges categorized by severity and psychological principle.

To ensure that 30-day analytics and heatmaps render instantly without stuttering the UI thread, we created compound B-tree indexes on `(habit_id, date)` and `(date)`. Furthermore, we enforced `ON DELETE CASCADE`: deleting a habit automatically purges associated logs and cached insights, maintaining zero orphaned records."


[SLIDE 6: BACKGROUND SERVICES & ANDROID WORKMANAGER]
Target Duration: ~1 minute 00 seconds

Key Emphasis Points:
• Address modern Android background constraints (Doze Mode, App Standby).
• Explain WorkManager isolate execution and the Streak Defense alert rule.

Verbatim Spoken Script:
"One of the most complex engineering challenges in modern Android development is running background tasks reliably without draining the device battery or being killed by Android's Doze mode.

Standard alarms and background threads are frequently terminated by the OS. To solve this, we implemented Android's recommended WorkManager API using Flutter's `@pragma('vm:entry-point')` callback dispatcher.

Every three hours, Android WorkManager wakes a background Dart isolate. This isolate independently initializes our SQLite database singleton and queries for any habits scheduled for today that remain incomplete.

If the user has an active streak of 3 or more days and the current time passes 6:00 PM, the background engine detects a 'Streak-at-Risk' condition. It immediately dispatches a high-priority local notification through our registered Android notification channel. This alerts the user to complete their 2-minute minimum viable habit before the day ends, actively preserving their momentum.

[SLIDE 7: RULE-BASED BEHAVIORAL ANALYTICS ENGINE]
Target Duration: ~1 minute 15 seconds

Key Emphasis Points:
• Explain why rule-based heuristics outperform heavy cloud LLMs for on-device habit tracking.
• Walk through the 4 heuristics: Habit Stacking, Weekend Slump, Circadian Friction, and Never Miss Twice.

Verbatim Spoken Script:
"Thank you, Aman. Good morning everyone. I will now present the intellectual engine of our project: the Rule-Based Behavioral Analytics Engine.

While large language models are powerful, running generative models in the cloud compromises privacy and incurs network latency, while running them on edge devices drains mobile batteries. We realized that behavioral psychology is fundamentally governed by deterministic laws. Therefore, we codified four major behavioral science heuristics into an on-device algorithmic engine:

First is James Clear's 'Habit Stacking'. The engine analyzes the user's 30-day consistency and identifies 'Anchor Habits' with over 75% consistency—such as your morning espresso. When you struggle with a new habit—like journaling at 35%—the engine pairs them, giving you an actionable formula: 'Immediately after Morning Espresso, write in your journal for 2 minutes.'

Second is BJ Fogg's 'Weekend Slump Detector'. The engine compares Monday-to-Friday completion against Saturday-and-Sunday completion. If consistency drops by more than 25%, it prescribes a 'Minimum Viable Habit'—reducing the target by 75% on weekends to preserve the neural habit loop without causing friction.

Third is Circadian Friction Analysis. By analyzing hourly check-in timestamps, the engine detects ego depletion—such as finding that morning workouts succeed 90% of the time, while evening workouts fail 65% of the time due to willpower exhaustion.

Fourth is the 'Never Miss Twice' Rule. If a habit was missed yesterday, the engine immediately elevates it to high priority before noon today, preventing a single lapse from turning into habit abandonment."
